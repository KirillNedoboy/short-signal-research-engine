# Evidence and Results

Public evidence is classified before interpretation:

- **Verified:** source behavior covered by committed tests; the test command and fixture names are the reproducibility boundary.
- **Historical:** dated reports or fixtures copied because they illustrate a contract. Their time window and denominator must be read before use.
- **Experimental:** V2/shadow or counterfactual work not established as live behavior.
- **UNKNOWN:** missing cohort coverage, unavailable timing, incomplete market fields, or an untested deployment claim.

The included low-volume report is a historical/counterfactual artifact, not a current performance report. The included replay fixture is a bounded sample, not a representative market cohort. No result should be interpreted as a profitability guarantee. Recompute metrics from the fixture and record the exact command, window, denominator, and exclusions when extending this evidence.
